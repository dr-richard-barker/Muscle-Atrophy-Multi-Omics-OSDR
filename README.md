# Muscle Atrophy Countermeasures (Multi-Omics & Nutrition)

Multi-omics meta-analysis of spaceflight-induced muscle atrophy integrated with connectivity mapping (LINCS L1000), molecular docking, and a plant-based nutritional countermeasure optimization framework.

## Overview

This repository hosts the data and analysis pipeline for re-deriving a consensus cross-species catabolic signature of spaceflight-induced muscle atrophy and translating these findings into evidence-based countermeasures. We integrate rodent and human spaceflight datasets from the NASA Open Science Data Repository (OSDR), perform connectivity mapping to identify counter-catabolic compounds, validate candidate nutraceuticals via molecular docking, and design optimized plant-based meal plans to meet muscle protein synthesis (MPS) thresholds.

## Key Results

- **Consensus Spaceflight Atrophy Signature**: Re-derived a robust 141-gene cross-species catabolic signature (77 up-regulated, 64 down-regulated) across 10 OSDR studies (rodent spaceflight, human astronaut cfRNA, Inspiration4 crew blood, and bed rest analogs).
- **Mitochondrial Dysfunction**: The spaceflight signature is heavily dominated by the up-regulation of mitochondrial electron transport chain (ETC) genes (e.g., *MT-ND1* through *MT-ND6*, *MT-CO2*, *MT-CYB*) and down-regulation of the master regulator *PPARGC1A/PGC-1α*.
- **Connectivity Mapping (LINCS L1000)**: Screened 559 compounds and identified **HMB, CoQ10, creatine, vitamin D3, and resveratrol** as top candidates for reversing the spaceflight catabolic transcriptional program.
- **Molecular Docking**: Validated binding affinities of candidate compounds against key protein targets (e.g., resveratrol against *SIRT1* and *FOXO3*; HMB against *mTOR*).
- **Vegan Protein Benchmarking**: Benchmarked 41 plant-based protein sources, identifying **pea protein isolate** (leucine 6.50g/100g, MPS score 2.25) and **soy protein isolate** (leucine 6.60g/100g, MPS score 2.11) as optimal sources for meeting the 2.5g leucine-per-meal threshold for MPS activation.

## Datasets

The meta-analysis incorporates 10 datasets from NASA OSDR and GEO:
- **Rodent Spaceflight**: OSD-21, OSD-111, OSD-135, OSD-401, GSE213808
- **Human Spaceflight**: OSD-684 (tissue chip), OSD-530 (astronaut cfRNA), OSD-569 (Inspiration4 blood transcriptomics), OSD-571 (Inspiration4 plasma proteomics)
- **Terrestrial Analog**: OSD-370 (9-day bed rest)

## Usage

All processed tables are available in the `tables/` directory. Figures can be viewed in the `figures/` folder. The raw data can be downloaded using the accessions listed in `manuscript/manuscript_npj_microgravity.md`.

Overview

Linking spaceflight muscle wasting to actionable dietary interventions.

Spaceflight-induced muscle atrophy remains a principal barrier to long-duration space exploration. We performed a cross-species random-effects meta-analysis of 10 transcriptomic datasets from the NASA Open Science Data Repository (OSDR), derived a consensus catabolic signature of 141 differentially expressed genes, and used connectivity mapping against LINCS L1000 and molecular docking to identify HMB, CoQ10, creatine, vitamin D3, and resveratrol as top counter-catabolic candidates.
